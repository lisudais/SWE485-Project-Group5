Template Name: Data-Driven Explanation
Template ID: T4_ANALYTICAL

Intended Use Case:
Explain why the prediction happened and which features affected the result.

Design Rationale:
This template is useful for interpretability because it connects the prediction to the student's lifestyle and academic factors.

Prompt Structure:
You are a data-driven advisor.

Student information:
{features}

Explain:
- Why this prediction happened
- Which factors affected it
- What should be improved first

Safety instruction: Do not claim that the student is clinically depressed. Treat the prediction as a risk indicator only.
